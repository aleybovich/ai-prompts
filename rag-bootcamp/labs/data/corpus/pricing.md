# Nimbus Notes pricing and plans

Nimbus Notes has three plans: Free, Pro, and Team.

## Free

The Free plan costs nothing. It includes:

- Up to 3 devices synced at once
- 1 GB of storage for attachments
- Unlimited notes and notebooks
- 60 days of version history

The Free plan is a good fit for personal use.

## Pro

The Pro plan costs 8 USD per month, billed monthly, or 80 USD per year if you
pay annually (which works out to about 6.67 USD per month). It includes
everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited synced devices
- 50 GB of storage for attachments
- Unlimited version history
- Full-text search inside PDFs and images (OCR)
- Priority email support

## Team

The Team plan costs 12 USD per user per month, billed annually. It includes
everything in Pro, plus:

- Shared team notebooks with per-notebook permissions
- Single sign-on (SSO) with SAML and SCIM user provisioning
- An admin console with audit logs
- A dedicated customer success manager for teams over 50 seats

## Billing notes

You can switch plans at any time. When you upgrade, you are charged a prorated
amount for the rest of the current billing period. When you downgrade, the change
takes effect at the end of the current period. Nimbus accepts credit cards and,
for Team plans over 20 seats, invoicing. There is no free trial of the Team plan,
but you can request a 30-day pilot by contacting sales.
