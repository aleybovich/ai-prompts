# Getting started with Nimbus Notes

This guide walks you through your first few minutes in Nimbus Notes.

## Create your account

Download Nimbus from nimbusnotes.com or install it from your app store. Open the
app and sign up with your email address and a password. Confirm your email by
clicking the link Nimbus sends you. You are now on the Free plan.

## Create your first note

Click the **New Note** button (or press `Cmd/Ctrl + N`). Give the note a title on
the first line, then start writing below it. Notes are written in Markdown, so
`# Heading`, `- bullet`, `**bold**`, and `` `code` `` all work. Nimbus saves as
you type — there is no save button.

## Organize with notebooks and tags

Every note lives in a notebook. Create a notebook from the sidebar, then drag
notes into it or pick a notebook when you create a note. Add tags by typing
`#tagname` anywhere in a note; tags let you group related notes across different
notebooks.

## Link notes together

Type `[[` to link to another note by title. Nimbus creates the connection and
shows a **backlink** on the target note, so from any note you can see everything
that references it. This is how people build a personal wiki inside Nimbus.

## Search

Press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` to open search from anywhere. Search matches note titles and
body text, and you can filter by notebook or tag. Results appear as you type
because Nimbus keeps a local search index on your device.

## Next steps

- Install Nimbus on your phone so notes follow you everywhere.
- Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings for extra account security.
- Explore keyboard shortcuts to move faster.
